Know the neighborhood

Small towns, big sky, and the best barbecue in Texas. Here's the Lee County Priscilla grew up in.

Giddings

The county seat on US-290, with the landmark 1899 courthouse, local schools, shopping and an easy run to Austin or Houston.

Lexington

Ranch country in the northwest corner, home of Snow's BBQ: twice named the best barbecue in Texas by Texas Monthly. Get there early on Saturday.

Dime Box

Named for the old custom of leaving a dime in a box for the mail run. Quiet roads, big pastures and a heritage museum full of stories.

Lake Somerville & Lincoln

The state park trailway, Nails Creek and Flag Pond: fishing, camping and hunting on the county's northeast edge.

Meet Priscilla

Rooted in this dirt.

Priscilla has lived on her family's farm in North Lee County her whole life. She graduated from Giddings High School, showed animals in the Lee County Junior Livestock Show, and worked as a licensed manicurist and a certified pharmacy technician right here in town before spending ten years in her family's oilfield business.

That oilfield decade is her secret weapon. Mineral rights, pipeline easements, lease roads and access: she has seen the paperwork from the other side of the table, and she knows which questions protect you.

When she isn't working she's riding the Ranger around the farm, chasing bugling elk, or fishing the coast with her husband Brandon and their two daughters. Her promise is simple: she will work hard to find your perfect piece of property, or help you find your forever home.
